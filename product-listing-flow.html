Visibility, enforced in one place

Every product query starts from the same predicate, so no route can forget it: 

products.status = 'active'
  AND products.deleted_at IS NULL
  AND products.published_at IS NOT NULL
  AND products.published_at <= now()

The last clause is the one worth noticing: a future published_at keeps a product invisible until its own launch time, with no scheduled job to run and nothing to forget to flip.

Worth a decision: stockQty is public

The response carries both stock (in / low / out) and stockQty, the exact available unit count summed across warehouses. It is in the published schema for both productSummary and productVariant, so it reaches any unauthenticated caller.

That contradicts the rule the codebase states for itself, in admin-inventory.stock.ts: "the storefront is told in / low / out, never a number. Publishing '3 left' invites scraping of the whole catalogue's stock position, and it is wrong the instant someone else's cart holds two of them." The stockState helper exists and is used — stockQty is simply published beside it.

Not something I changed: removing a field is a breaking change for whatever the storefront already renders, and that is your call rather than mine. Flagging it so the choice is deliberate.
